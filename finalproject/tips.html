Beginner Gardening Tips

  • Choose plants suited for small spaces.
  • Use containers with proper drainage.
  • Water consistently but avoid overwatering.
  • Maximize sunlight with window boxes or balconies.
  • Compost kitchen scraps for natural fertilizer.
  • Start with easy plants like herbs and leafy greens.
  • Rotate pots occasionally so all sides get sunlight.
  • Group plants with similar watering needs together.
  • Use vertical space — hanging pots or shelves save room.
  • Harvest regularly to encourage new growth.
  • Check soil moisture with your finger before watering.
  • Clean leaves occasionally to help plants absorb light.
  • Repot plants when roots outgrow their containers.
  • Keep a simple gardening journal to track progress.
  • Be patient — gardening is a process of learning and growth.
